Important safeguards and limitations
  • Videos are tracked by their unique YouTube video IDs.
  • Positive Voice marks a video as commented only after YouTube confirms publication through this app.
  • Comments posted outside this app are not automatically verified in this version.
  • Bulk preparation creates distinct drafts. Review each draft before publishing. Batch approval is a single confirmation for all visible pending drafts; check the video titles and comment text first.
  • YouTube search and statistics are subject to API availability and daily quota.
