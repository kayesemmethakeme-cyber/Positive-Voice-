Privacy and publishing controls
  • Comments are submitted only after explicit approval.
  • Access tokens remain in this browser session.
  • Do not share API keys or OAuth credentials.
  • Server history requires persistent storage configured by the hosting administrator.
\n
Important safeguards and limitations
  • Videos are tracked by their unique YouTube video IDs.
  • SUHAIS marks a video as commented only after YouTube confirms publication through this app.
  • Comments posted outside this app are not automatically verified in this version.
  • Bulk preparation creates distinct drafts. Review each draft before publishing. Batch approval is a single confirmation for all visible pending drafts; check the video titles and comment text first.
  • YouTube search and statistics are subject to API availability and daily quota.

ABOUT THE APPLICATION

SUHAIS

Voice Your Heart

07

A thoughtful space to discover ideas, express perspectives and engage in meaningful conversations.

Discover

Find YouTube videos by subject, explore new perspectives and organize discoveries with practical search and filtering tools.

Express

Turn your central message into relevant, individual comments. Choose a simple writing approach or use advanced controls for tone, language, attitude and form.

Review & publish

Review, refine and approve every comment before publishing through your own connected YouTube account.

Learn from your activity

Keep track of discovered videos, drafts, published comments and revisions, with clear activity statistics.

Our principles

  • Authenticity: comments should express your actual perspective.
  • Relevance: adapt each comment to the video context available.
  • Respect: disagree thoughtfully and constructively.
  • Control: AI prepares drafts; you decide what gets published.
  • Transparency: distinguish recorded submissions from comments confirmed publicly visible.

SUHAIS is an independent application and is not affiliated with YouTube or Google. YouTube API availability, platform rules and quotas apply.
